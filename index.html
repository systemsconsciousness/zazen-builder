Our First Coast Roots

Forty-Eight Seasons of Salt, Sand & Atlantic Swell

In 1978, Salt Pier Surf Supply opened its doors three blocks north of the Jacksonville Beach Pier. We were founded on a straightforward principle: North Florida waves demand thoughtful board shapes crafted specifically for our shallow sandbars and shifting tide swings.

From hurricane swells spinning off Cape Hatteras to mellow summer knees on the south bar, our shapers and coaches have mentored three generations of First Coast surfers. We believe in honest craftsmanship, ocean respect, and keeping the authentic soul of Jax Beach alive.
